About

I work at the intersection of inequality research and strategic foresight — using causal designs to find where advantage is transmitted, and building the data and scenarios that let institutions act before it consolidates.

From Almería, one of the sunniest cities in Europe.

You can read more about me here More about me ↓

Now

Deputy Director at the Directorate for Public Policy, Presidency of the Government of Spain. I coordinate a cross-ministerial team and lead the analytical work behind priority reforms: the 2026 extraordinary regularisation, the Plan de Integración y Ciudadanía, and the organic law on the protection of minors in digital environments.

Before

Senior Policy Analyst at the National Office of Foresight & Strategy, where I built the two instruments that now power my research: Generaciones del Futuro, HispanIA 2040.

Trained

PhD in Political and Social Sciences at the European University Institute (2023), with Fabrizio Bernardi. Postdoc at UNED within MapIneq, Research Fellow at INVEST (Turku), Visiting Scholar at Wisconsin–Madison. I teach causal inference at RECSM (UPF) every summer.

01 ↘ Research area 01 / 04

Educational inequalities

Family origin still shapes who makes it through school — but not everywhere, and rarely through the channels we assume. I use causal designs to locate the moments at which education systems either transmit advantage or interrupt it.

02 ↘ Research area 02 / 04

Work & labour markets

Automation, AI and credential rules are redrawing who works, at what, and for how much. I study who is exposed to that restructuring at labour-market entry, and for whom exposure turns into a wage or mobility penalty.

04 ↘ Research area 04 / 04

Foresight & indicators

Inequality that is not measured cannot be governed. I build the cohort data, the indicators and the scenarios that let institutions see inequality forming — years before it shows up in the outcomes we usually track.
